## Summary

Combines the grand tour (dense path through projection space) with projection pursuit (optimization of a "interestingness" index) into a "projection pursuit guided tour." The PP index steers the tour toward interesting projections, assisting in cluster detection and finding low-dimensional structure. Published in J Computational & Graphical Statistics.

## Relevance to This Work

**Our clue-guided exploration is the latent anamorph analog of the guided tour:**

- Guided tour: PP index steers toward interesting projections → our clue channel steers toward the privileged projection
- Guided tour: structure is discovered in existing data → our viewer: structure is discovered in designed objects
- The tour taxonomy (grand = exhaustive, guided = steered, manual = user-controlled) maps to our viewer modes (random = grand, hint = guided, drag = manual)

Could frame our contribution partly as: "a new member of the tour family, where the guidance signal comes from a designed clue channel rather than a statistical index, and the structure being sought is intentionally embedded rather than naturally occurring."
