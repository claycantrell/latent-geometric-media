## Summary

Proposes a method for rooting watermarks in latent diffusion models. The watermark is embedded in the decoder of the model's autoencoder, so every image generated by the model carries an invisible but detectable watermark. Published at ICCV 2023.

## Key Findings

- Watermark is embedded in the latent space of a pretrained autoencoder (VAE decoder fine-tuned)
- Every generated image carries the watermark without visible quality degradation
- Robust against image transformations (crop, resize, JPEG compression)
- Detection via a trained extractor network

## Relevance to This Work

**Closest steganography precedent for "structure in latent space."** But the goals diverge sharply:

- They embed a *hidden* binary signature → we embed a *legible* form
- Their emphasis is *concealment* (invisible to humans) → our emphasis is *designed legibility* (visible under the right projection)
- They have no concept of multiple views, polysemy, or decoys
- They embed in a learned latent space → we construct the space itself
- Their watermark is recovered by a trained neural network → our forms are recovered by geometric projection

The distinction is: steganography hides information; anamorphosis reveals form to the right observer. Same mathematical substrate (latent representations), opposite design goals.
